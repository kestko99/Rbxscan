🎛️ Control Panel Features:

  • Status: Shows if extractor is active and working
  • Last Check: Timestamp of most recent cookie check
  • Cookies Found: Counter of how many cookies sent
  • Stop Button: Click to stop the auto-extractor

⚠️ Important Notes:

  • The extractor checks for cookies every 5 seconds
  • It only runs when you're on roblox.com pages
  • A control panel appears in the top-left corner when active
  • Click "Stop" in the control panel to disable it
  • Your cookies are sent to your Discord webhook automatically
